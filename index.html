About CampusX

About CampusX

CampusX is a student-driven platform created to make learning, collaboration and resource sharing easier for engineering students. Instead of students searching across multiple platforms for notes, roadmaps, project ideas, teammates and study material, CampusX brings these student needs into one organized ecosystem.

Vision: Build a stronger student community where knowledge is shared, opportunities are discovered, and students grow together.
